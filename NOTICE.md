# Attribution

Jellyfin Partyline is a fork of [Syncplay Chat](https://github.com/AbhayVAshokan/jellyfin-syncplay-chat) by Abhay V Ashokan.

The fork preserves the original SyncPlay text-chat functionality and adds opt-in WebRTC voice chat, persistent history, mobile controls, connection recovery, and private HTTPS deployment guidance. It is independently maintained by [Llewellyn500](https://github.com/Llewellyn500).

The original Git history and GNU General Public License v3 are retained.
